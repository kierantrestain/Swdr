## Arc Studio

| Check | Documented steps and behaviour | SWDR decision | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ARC-01 Season to episode | Create a season outline and bible. Add an episode column and beats. Use the episode menu to create its script. Work in the episode Beats view, then return to the season bible to see the episode changes reflected. [Season Outlines & Bibles](https://help.arcstudiopro.com/guides/season-outlines-bibles) | Give season, episode, document and beat their own stable identities. Treat the season overview and its episodes as linked views. | A two-season fixture has 24 episodes. Edit an episode title or beat and confirm the same episode and beat identity in the overview after reopening. |
| ARC-02 Beat to scene | Outline acts and beats in Plot Board. Review the beat order in Outline and expose its context alongside the script. A beat can coexist with a written scene and be linked to the manuscript. [Plot Board and Outline](https://help.arcstudiopro.com/guides/the-plot-board) | Link an outline beat to a stable scene ID. Provide a deliberate route from beat to script and preserve the link when the scene moves. | Link to scene B, move the scene above A, follow the beat link and confirm the same heading and script source. |
| ARC-03 Focus | Enter using the Focus control or the documented keyboard shortcut. Most surrounding controls disappear, with a route back to normal editing. [Focus](https://help.arcstudiopro.com/how-tos/viewing-settings/how-do-i-start-writing-in-focus-mode) | Keep the editing surface mounted when entering Focus. Escape returns to the workspace with the original caret and scroll. | Place the caret mid-line on a scrolled page, enter Focus, type, exit with Escape and verify position, selection and typed text. |
| ARC-04 Alternate draft and feedback | Create a named alternate draft from the draft list. It starts private and can be unhidden. Add a comment to selected script text, jump from the comments list to its location, and resolve the thread after review. [Private version](https://help.arcstudiopro.com/how-tos/feedback-and-collaboration/how-do-i-create-a-private-version-of-my-draft) · [Collaboration](https://help.arcstudiopro.com/guides/collaboration-feedback) | Offer named drafts and explicit share or issue. Keep personal work outside team visibility until shared. Anchor feedback to the revision and block. | A private draft stays absent from another participant's library. A shared draft accepts scoped comments. An editor resolves a thread, and its original source remains available. |
| ARC-05 Stash recovery | Select text, add it to the Stash, optionally name it, preview it and drag it back into the manuscript. [Stash](https://help.arcstudiopro.com/guides/the-stash) | Keep stashes recoverable after restart. Restore into a new head and retain source material. | Save a named stash, reopen offline, restore and inspect both the new head and the retained original. |

## StudioBinder

| Check | Documented steps and behaviour | SWDR decision | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SB-01 Revised script intake | Sync the screenplay to the project, review the impact, name the revision, compare versions and apply. The help warns that changed scene numbers or headings can replace linked work in the current view and describes rollback. [Revision sync](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/12023533-how-to-sync-a-revised-script) | Start from an issued revision. Preview moved, added, omitted, edited and unresolved scenes, preserve stable mapped links, and require a decision for ambiguous ones. Keep the older source revision. | Reimport a revision with a moved scene, a modified heading, a new scene and an omission. Confirm the review never silently transfers ambiguous tags or shots, mapped rows keep their identities, and the earlier revision opens intact. |
| SB-02 Breakdown and sides | Tag a scene's cast, props, costume or other elements. Derive a report and choose sides by scene, character or shooting day. [Professional features](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/10951295-what-s-included-with-the-professional-plan) | Keep breakdown items linked to the released scene. Build sides from the selected scoped script and shoot day. | Filter to a selected cast member or day, export sides and compare the scene set, cast and released revision ID against the production calendar. |
| SB-03 Shots and storyboard | Import the screenplay and confirm the detected scene set. Build shot lists with framing, specs and images, then generate boards from the same scene work. [Storyboard from a script](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/10334726-how-to-create-a-storyboard-with-a-script) · [Professional features](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/10951295-what-s-included-with-the-professional-plan) | Give shots stable IDs and keep storyboard assets attached to shot and scene sources. Use the same records for the shot list and lookbook. | Add a frame and camera note. Reorder the shots and import a moved scene in a released revision. The attachment and the shot's source remain correct after reload. |
| SB-04 Schedule | Arrange scene strips with shoot-day boundaries and company moves. Derive scheduling reports and day-out-of-days. [Professional features](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/10951295-what-s-included-with-the-professional-plan) | Derive unit schedules, cast conflicts, work days and cost reports from dated scene strips and team availability. | Move a strip causing overlapping cast calls. See the conflict and correct it. Check the resulting work-day count and cost totals against the fixture. |
| SB-05 Call sheet | Create a call sheet from a day break and review the populated layout. Preview the sheet, select recipients and confirm delivery. Track viewed and confirmed status. Reissue after a call-time update. [Day break](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/419954-how-to-create-a-call-sheet-from-your-stripboard) · [Delivery](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/6723823-how-to-send-a-call-sheet) · [Confirmations](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/6742381-how-to-track-call-sheet-views-and-confirmations) · [Resend](https://support.studiobinder.com/en/articles/6742363-how-to-reset-call-sheet-confirmations) | Freeze a dated issue on preview and send a test delivery to a synthetic recipient. Keep confirmations scoped to the issue. | Acknowledge issue 1, update the call time and issue version 2. Issue 1 keeps its old time and acknowledgement. Issue 2 requires its own acknowledgement and records its source revision. |

## OpenDraft

| Check | Documented steps and behaviour | SWDR decision | Pass condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OD-01 Boot and writing | The pinned release provides desktop packages. The source install guide describes a local build served at `localhost:8000`. The feature guide documents typed screenplay elements, navigator and keyboard fall-through rules. [Release](https://github.com/Proteus-Technologies-Private-Limited/OpenDraft/releases/tag/v2.13.0) · [Pinned features](https://github.com/Proteus-Technologies-Private-Limited/OpenDraft/blob/41fd8b90c22309729f807f388d36b16d700eea38/docs/FEATURES.md) | Recreate the practical keyboard and navigation behaviour in the SWDR editor. Gate reuse of upstream components on observed runtime compatibility and licensing. | Launch the exact baseline in a clean local profile and type and export a synthetic script. In SWDR, type heading, action, character, parenthetical, and dialogue using the intended keystrokes. |
| OD-02 AV timing | Create a two-column script, enter content and durations, then insert or move rows. The manual describes calculated start times, an optional frame column and spreadsheet and structured YAML exports. [Manual](https://proteus-technologies-private-limited.github.io/OpenDraft/user-manual/index.html) | Keep row identity, cue time and storyboard image together through row moves. Distinguish structured AV exports from lossy tables. | Reorder rows with two timing overrides in a 60-second cut and a 20-minute master. Verify displayed totals, export and reimport supported fields and identify losses. |
| OD-03 Characters and relationships | Create character profiles, add relationships in the Characters panel and inspect the relationship map. [Characters](https://proteus-technologies-private-limited.github.io/OpenDraft/user-manual/characters.html) · [Manual](https://proteus-technologies-private-limited.github.io/OpenDraft/user-manual/index.html) | Independently record an appearance, co-presence or interaction with its source. Add evolving relationship and knowledge states with story and reveal order. | Compare relationship state before and after the same event, distinguish the three forms of participation and trace the event to its exact retained line. Verify spoiler scoping in viewers and data queries. |
| OD-04 History and offline | Check in a draft, compare revisions, restore an earlier version and recover a local backup. Desktop install bundles its local runtime. [History](https://proteus-technologies-private-limited.github.io/OpenDraft/user-manual/version-history.html) · [Backup and recovery](https://proteus-technologies-private-limited.github.io/OpenDraft/user-manual/backups.html) | Persist edits on the device and display team sync independently. Restore makes a new head. | Disconnect, edit, close and reopen. Recover unacknowledged edits locally, reconnect and compare the accepted head and saved snapshot. |
| OD-05 Co-writing | Create a session, invite another writer, choose access and edit together with cursor presence. Manual and pinned dependencies document a distinct collaboration path. [Collaboration](https://proteus-technologies-private-limited.github.io/OpenDraft/user-manual/collaboration.html) · [Pinned frontend dependencies](https://github.com/Proteus-Technologies-Private-Limited/OpenDraft/blob/41fd8b90c22309729f807f388d36b16d700eea38/frontend/package.json) | Keep role checks on the server. Use durable operations and retain conflicts for review. | Two writers edit separate then overlapping text, disconnect and recover. Revocation denies new reads, writes, asset downloads and presence at the configured scope. |
| OD-06 Format fidelity | Use native Save As or import and export conventional screenplay formats. The public manual documents limitations and confirms `.celtx` and `.wdz` as import-only there. [Format compatibility](https://proteus-technologies-private-limited.github.io/OpenDraft/user-manual/format-compatibility.html) · [Pinned native schema](https://github.com/Proteus-Technologies-Private-Limited/OpenDraft/blob/41fd8b90c22309729f807f388d36b16d700eea38/frontend/src/utils/odraftFormat.ts) | Retain original bytes, preview editable fields and detect unsupported inputs. Keep proprietary adapters outside the supported list until genuine permitted exports pass checks. | Restore one exact-release `.odraft` with assets, then import and export each supported format for the published field matrix. Record source and converted page/scene totals, scene anchors, typed lines, dual dialogue and explicit losses. |
